Back To School Fashion

The beginning of the school year is like a new chapter of a book. You have no idea what will happen, but you meet new characters (friends and teachers) and go to new places (new classroom)! And your clothing can make a huge statement, so let's start!

Rules

Fashion is another way to express your personality, but we also have to be mindful about our choices. Read your school dress code. Some common rules are; must have shoulder straps, no rips above your mid-thigh, non- revealing, and appropriate for school. Even if you don't have those rules, still be mindful of what your wearing. Before you pick, ask yourself a few questions. Is it appropriate? Does it show violence? Does it show too much skin? Is it even comfy?

Ideas

Some fashion ideas that are simple and chic include jeans, oversized T shirts, light cargo pants, and cardigans! They are comfy but also follow most rules. For hair, make sure it doesn't distract you. Don't color your hair bright pink or something like that. Keep it simple; claw clip, rubber band, or headbands work fine.

Stores

When looking for fashion, one size doesn't fit all. Different stores are four different aesthetics. If you want plain designer, H&M is right for you! If you are more classic or vintage, go to Abercrombie & Fitch. Looking for something fashionably comfy? Say no more; GAP! Teen clothing? Hollister Co. But anyways, find your true style and let it shine!
