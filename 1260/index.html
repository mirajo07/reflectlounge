Pack up for India in December

Are you going to India this winter? You're in the right place!

Me and my family are going to Uttarakhand, in the north.

1. Clothes

◼Pants, such as jeans or fuzzy leggings.

◼Long sleeved shirts of sweaters

◼Very Heavy Jackets, like Patagonia, Spider, or Columbia. Quality Matters!!!

Shoes

◼Hiking shoes. It's the mountains, anyways!

◼Sometimes, pack boots.

Bathroom

◼Makeup, if you really need it

◼Toothpaste and Brush

◼Lotion- its cold, and don't crack your skin!
