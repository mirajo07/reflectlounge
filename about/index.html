My Story

Like most as a child, I had a diary/journal. In my head, I thought I could somehow get into a “coma and forget about my entire life” (sounds funny, but that was me as a seven-year old). Honestly, I never actually wrote anything about my life in those. When I was around nine years old, my parents encouraged me to create a portfolio of my experiences. It sounded like a cool idea at first, but during the beginning, I didn’t really like blogging because it felt like homework, and I wasn’t good at it anyways. But after a few years to now, after I started to enjoy it much more, The Hobby Lounge has become a portfolio, a journal, a guide for students, a bunch of articles, a book of life mistakes, and most importantly, how YOU can make YOUR life better.
