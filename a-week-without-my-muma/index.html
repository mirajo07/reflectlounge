A week without my muma :=-(

Its really hard for me to survive with my mom on a business trip 300 miles away FOR A WEEK. Even though its sad, you can learn a lot of stuff in that one week. Here are some things you can learn.

Sleep in Your Room

I usually sleep with my mom because the bed is softer. But this week I had to sleep in my own room. Before now, I never felt comfortable. This time, I can sleep in my room with a M light and slept thoroughly.

Cooking Food

My paapa was the one cooking the food, and I learned the DON'Ts of cooking. DON'T overcook anything. DON'T add too much water in a soaked dish. DON'T over roast and then soak anything. DON'T add too less salt. DON'T not make sure there is an equal balance between liquid and solid in a gravy or sauce. Those are some simple cooking tips.

Patience!

I was patient enough to wait for my muma for a week! If you can do that, you can be patient anywhere. Be mindfull and think about good stuff like ice cream while your being patient for the dentist and more.
