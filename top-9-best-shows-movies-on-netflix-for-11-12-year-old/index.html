Top 9 Best Shows/ Movies On Netflix For 9-10 Year Old

I love Netflix, but I can never find a good, popular show! Either it's too cringy, or too boring, or just terrible. But I'm picky, and maybe you are too! After watching a bunch of TV shows, I have narrowed down the truly BEST picks!

  1. Geek Girl

This show is so good! A normal girl, Harriet Manners becomes a fashion model. But many people try to pull her back, like Poppy, Jude Paignton, and her school, where everyone hates her. She also has to handle friends, family, school, and modeling at the same time! She even skipped school to try out for modeling. But... on the second last episode, she gets fired from her job. This is my favorite show!!! You must watch it.

2 . I Woke Up A Vampire

On Carmie's 13th birthday, she finds out that she is a vampire! She has to hide this from her parents, who adopted her. But her siblings find out anyways. She tells her friends, and she is on a hunt to find her real mother. But her mother was a kidnapper and a murder, who used to steal magical children and put them in jars. She also has to run away from a 'friend' who hunts down magical beings too!

3. The A List

This show is really dramatic! Mia is a perfect girl who goes to a camp called Peregrine Island, where many mysteries hide. Amber is another camper who has the magical power to manipulate people into thinking false thoughts. She can manipulate everyone; except Mia. But Amber was actually a lab experiment gone wrong, and Midge, her true self, is revealed to be evil. Are you ready for the thrill?

4. Wednesday

Wednesday Addams is a dark teen, who attends Nevermore Academy, is roommates with a kind and colorful werewolf named Enid. Wednesday doesn't want friends, but she finds out it hurts to not have friends. She also uncovers the secrets of her town, her family, and in return, kills her evil teacher who killed the principle of the school, and sets fire to the town.

5. Young Sheldon

There are many long seasons in this family comedy, and every episode is worth it! Sheldon Cooper is a child genius, and he started college at the age of 11! His older brother creates a business and her sister is the average tween magazine- addict. But his family is CRAZY, and your mind will be perplexed after you laugh out all of your laughs on this show!

6. The Baby- Sitters Club

Based off the books by Ann M Martin, plus many other artists much as Gale Galligan and Raina Telgemeier, this hit show includes Kristy, Mary Ann, Stacey, and Claudia on a quest to create a big baby sitters business! But competitors, Stacey's health problems, and Kristy's moving makes everything different.

7. Rotten

This is real life stuff, like how food is made, the industry cheating on consumers, and so much more! This is just a really great show you MUST watch. It is also a good study resource. If your teacher asks you to write an essay about a REAL movie or show, just write about this one!

8. Alexa & Katie

Alexa, the bubbly one with cancer, and Katie, the kind one that helps Alexa everywhere. These inseparable besties, no matter their differences, will face any challenge, and show what friendship looks like! Each episode is different, with Katie attending a big career event in London, Alexa getting cancer, and Katie shaving her head off to support Alexa. This show is super good but also sad. :(
