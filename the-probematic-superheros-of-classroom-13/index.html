The probematic superheros of classroom 13

The Super Awful Superheroes of Classroom 13; the funniest book ever! A class gets struck by lightning... and get amazing, questionable superpowers! Each chapter has a different fellow classmate and their terrible superpower. From shape-shifting twins to a weird dolphin, everyone will find the characters very concerning! But Fatima discovers something terrible; her classmates will fight and destroy the world (or their teachers globe)!

The not-so honest Honest Lee and Matthew J. Gilbert form the Classroom 13 alliance! It is a very exciting and funny book series. A very unlucky classroom of lottery winners, magical, and famous hold their record for very unlucky and disastrous students and teacher. This is the 4th book of this fun class!

While you read this book, you will endure in friend drama, villains, and giant robots! Create your alliance and fight for your freedom! Read this book now!
