My Own BOOK

The Battle Of Torra

This is a spectacular book written by Miraya Joshi (Me!) at the age of 9.

This book has 79 pages and the grammar really isn't complex at all, but maybe think twice about how hard understanding the plot is!

The book is about a young 5th grade girl named Rita who embarks the adventure of her life through all sorts of insanely magical detentions, meeting evil- and kind hearted friends along the way to save her innocent best friend from dying.

The moment you set your eyes on this book, you know that it is your perfect fit.

Likes... & Dislikes

Okay, so what I like about my book is that it has a good moral- kindness saves the day. Also, unlike other books, the wording is nice and easy-to-read so that almost ANY age can read and enjoy it.

Now, what I don't like about my book is that THERE ARE SO MANY CHARACTERS!!! I guess the characters just decided to jump out at random times. The plot is also crazy, but if you read it slowly and try understand it, you would get it.

Since I'm basically the author of this book, its going to be a little hard to do likes and dislikes.
