Hiding from the Danger Forever

The Diary Of A Young Girl

Information You Ought to Know

This non-fiction diary wrote by Anne Frank, a young Jewish girl is so inspiring! This book is not part of any series and is a masterpiece on its own. It is 240 pages long with occasional pictures. Anne Frank was hiding from the Nazi at the time, and these are all of her thoughts with friends and family, life, and grief.

Plot... Not Spoiled

While Anne is living a fine life, she was to move away to hide from her biggest enemy; The Nazi. Will she live to tell the tale?

Characters... Not Spoiled

Anne lives with her parents, sister, and some of her fathers friends in an office to call home while the Nazi are hunting.

Really, just imagine; You are living in an office for years, hiding in office times and coming out for night, hoping not to get caught while your family is being sent to concentration camps!

It is such a magical experience to read her words of determination in the hardest of times.

Strait To The Opinion

Absolutely a five star book! This book is really good for anyone who wants to hear words and opinions from a real person experiencing a major war. I like her writing style. It is in a simple language that anyone will most likely understand. The beginning of the story is kind of plain, but through the book, suspense builds up into a great book!
