Sweet Potato gratin! (So easy)

I love making food, and this super easy sweet potato gratin will make your mouth water! So warm, soft, and cheesy, this casserole-like meal will indulge you in a savory, soft bite that will last forever. Start making this 6 ingredient recipe NOW!

Ingredients:

Large bowl of chopped sweet potatoes

2 TBSP of fresh thyme

2 TBSP of salt and pepper

1 Cup Gouda cheese

3/4 Cup Parmesan cheese

3/4 Cup Milk

3/4 Cup Heavy whipping cream

In a large, oven safe skillet, grease it and evenly place potatoes until they cover the bottom. Then, add both the cheese on top, evenly. Add a generous amount of salt, pepper, and thyme. In a different bowl, mix milk and heavy whipping cream. Mix together and drizzle a bit onto the top of the potatoes. Repeat many times the layering and when it reaches the top, and submerge into milk mixture. Bake covered for 30 minutes, or until browned on the top. Wait for 10 minutes before serving, and finally, enjoy with family and friends!
