Life in Dallas

A few days ago, I moved to Dallas, TX from Austin. This is actually a very huge change in my life because the DFW airport, which is the second- largest airport in the USA! That means more flights, better lounges, and better international connectivity. Another great thing about Dallas is the schools. The schools in Dallas have very high rankings, so yay for better education! And something extremely different about Dallas compared to Austin is the houses. Austin is one of the fastest- growing cities in Texas, so newer homes. But MY new home is Dallas is over 40 years old! At first I was traumatized about moving... again. But now that I'm here, I love the greenery, library, and surroundings. I also love art, so this area is a beautiful area to draw and paint plants, trees, and birds. So moving isn't the worst thing ever! This move covers my creativity, studies, peace, and overall health. I hope this is a reminder that moving can have benifits!!!
