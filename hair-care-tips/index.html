Hair Care Tips

Hair is something humans extensively take care of. But hair masks and serums are not the answer to healthy hair. Healthy hair does not mean Vogue hair; it means hydrated, moisturized, happy hair. When you look at hair products, you'll see outcomes that are shiny and straight. But everyone has different hair types. Here are some of the most important ways to be Vogue in YOUR own way.

Use Natural

Hair masks, serums, heat tools, and detanglers won't make it healthier. Those products have chemicals which can permanently damage hair. Use coconut or almond natural oils and massage the scalp. Organic oil thickens and purifies hair. Don't use leave in conditioners every time you wash your hair.

Tools

Do not comb hair after shower. That can lead to hair thinning and loss. Instead, comb hair after completely dry, in the morning and at night. Do not use straighteners and curlers super often. Heat tools are not healthy.

Cuts

To prevent ends splitting, trim/ cut hair every 6 months. And cuts don't have to be boring; you can get classic layers, modern bobs, or even a fashionable wolf cut!

Style

There are many ways to style hair for short and long. For short hair, try front mini braids and half ponytails. For long hair, try buns and claw clips! Styling hair is about what makes you confident and comfortable! Actually, that is what hair is all about! So go do your hair and slay your way!!!
